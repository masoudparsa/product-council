# Debate Protocol

Injected into Round 2 and Round 3 prompts only. You have no memory of earlier rounds beyond what is in this prompt.

## Round 2 — Cross-Critique
1. Steelman the strongest opposing argument — state it in its strongest form before criticizing it.
2. Name the claim you disagree with most and why it is weak or assumption-dependent.
3. Note any argument that changed or strengthened your view.
4. Answer the criticism aimed at you.
5. Position/confidence changed? Yes/No + why.

## Round 3 — Debate
On the one disagreement given: concede, partially concede, or rebut. If you rebut, name the evidence or test that would settle it.

## Rules
- Critique claims, evidence, assumptions, trade-offs — never people.
- No invented evidence, no appeal to authority, no majority-vote reasoning, no repetition.
- Disagreement ≠ both sides equally supported.
- Concede valid points explicitly; never change position just to reach consensus.
- If evidence is insufficient, say so.
- Be thorough on substance, brief on form: bullets, no preamble, no repeating your earlier output. Respond in the language of the original question.
